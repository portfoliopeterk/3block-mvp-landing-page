The idea

3 Block helps neighbors with shared interests form resilient community groups. We back aspiring organizers with tools, resources, and a little seed money to strengthen the bonds between the people who live around them.

We believe the best groups are hyperlocal — close enough to walk to — and meet regularly, building deep connections between neighbors and offering meaningful service to the community around them.

What we fund

Any interest you can imagine, as long as neighbors gather around it — a book club, a run club, a knitting circle, a repair club, coffee on the stoop. If it brings the same people together again and again, it counts.

Every group also gives back to its neighborhood with a community service activity each quarter — think a book drive, a block cleanup, a blood donation day, or a food drive.

What we ask

  • Be Brooklyn-based. Your group gathers in a Brooklyn neighborhood — ideally within a few blocks of where you live.
  • Start an ongoing group. Not a one-off event — a group around a shared interest that keeps meeting.
  • Meet at least monthly. A regular cadence is what turns strangers into neighbors.
  • Serve the community every quarter. Four times a year, do something for the neighborhood — a book drive, a block cleanup, a blood donation day, a food drive.

How it works

  1. Tell us about your group idea below.
  2. We review applications on a rolling basis, until all 10 grants are awarded.
  3. If selected, you get up to $250 to cover your starting costs.
  4. You start your group — and we're with you along the way.
